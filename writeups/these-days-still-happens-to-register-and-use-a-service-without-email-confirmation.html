These days still happens to register and use a service without email confirmation

Published: 10.06.2026

Been awarded with another bounty for getting a list of company employees using an LMS for learning and training.

The LMS allowed registering users with specific domain emails, company owned domain. So I decided to register with sumbru@<company>.com. Right after registration I was able to login to LMS and list all of the trainings, learning points, certificates and the Leaderboard. The Leaderboard was a list of employees with their usernames, emails and some other details. I reported this finding. The issue was fixed, now you have to confirm email accessing a link sent to the same email in order to finish registration.

The impact of this finding is that:

  • Malicious actors can create highly personalized phishing or social engineering campaigns by targeting specific employees identified through their email address.
  • Having a valid email or username makes brute-force password attacks more efficient, as attackers only need to focus on guessing valid passwords for existing users.
  • Knowing which user email exists is a crucial step for attackers seeking to gain access to user or employee accounts within the system(s).
